About Mug & Meadow

❧ ❧ ❧

Mug & Meadow was created as a gentle place to explore spirituality without pressure, performance or exaggerated promises.

The focus is on curiosity, reflection and learning together. You do not need experience, special abilities or all the answers. You only need an open mind and a willingness to participate.

The Circle

Beginner Spiritual Development Circle

A small in-person circle for people interested in developing their intuition and exploring spiritual connection in a grounded, welcoming environment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need experience?

No. The circle is intended for beginners and people who are still exploring what spirituality means to them.

Is the circle religious?

No. Mug & Meadow is not connected to a religion or faith organization.

Where is it held?

The circle is held in Cambridge, Ontario. Full location details are provided to registered participants.

Is this a private reading?

No. This is a small group development circle rather than a one-to-one reading.

How many people attend?

The group is intentionally kept small to create a comfortable and supportive experience.
